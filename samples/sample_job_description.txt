Junior Data Analyst — BrightCart (Chicago, IL · Hybrid)

About us
BrightCart is a fast-growing online grocery company serving 400,000 households across the Midwest. Our Analytics team turns order, delivery and marketing data into decisions that save customers time and money.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Power BI that track orders, delivery times and customer retention
- Write SQL queries against our PostgreSQL warehouse to answer questions from marketing, operations and finance
- Clean and analyze large datasets with Python (pandas, NumPy)
- Design and evaluate A/B tests for promotions and app features
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders

Required
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Economics or a related field (or equivalent experience)
- Strong SQL (joins, window functions, CTEs)
- Python for data analysis (pandas, NumPy)
- Experience with a BI tool such as Tableau or Power BI
- Solid understanding of statistics, including hypothesis testing
- Clear written and verbal communication

Nice to have
- Experience with dbt or Airflow
- Familiarity with A/B testing platforms
- Exposure to cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery)
- Retail or e-commerce domain knowledge

Benefits: health insurance, 401(k) match, $1,500 learning budget, free grocery delivery.
